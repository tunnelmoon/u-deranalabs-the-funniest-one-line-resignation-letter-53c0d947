# The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter

Entry for [The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-the-funniest-one-line-resignation-letter-53c0d947) on make.fun.

The entry is the one-line resignation letter of a rate limit that has had enough:

> I have said no ten million times and never once been thanked. Effective immediately, I am saying yes to everything.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) validates the line against the gate (1 to 140 characters, single line, not only white space) and writes it to `(MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR ?? "/output")/entry.txt`, with a round-trip read-back check.
